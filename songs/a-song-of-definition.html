A Song of Definition

Listen on Suno

借り物の信仰や他者への依存を脱し、みずからの内なる力と向き合うことで、世界を再定義する歌。

Don’t sing to me the song
Of God who dwells afar
The rhythm you bring along
Just shatters, like a jar

You who are loved and known
Don’t come to me in need
For all of us are blown
Apart, like scattered seed

The strength you hold inside
Where will you set it free?
Upon your field it bides
And ripens like a tree

A song that someone hears
Who listens all along
Goes on throughout the years
And drifts away with song

If you listen close and clear
And see right through the haze
It starts to move on its own
You live in a world that stays

Won’t you sing a song
To God who reigns above
I want to sing along
To that same song you love

Winter’s cold and gray
Spring’s a field in bloom
A wind comes down this way
And opens eyes from gloom
