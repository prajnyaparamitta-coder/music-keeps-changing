Watching the Castle Tremble

Listen on Suno

水面に揺れる城影を見つめながら、不確かな世界をどう受け止めるかはすべて自分の眼差し次第であることに気づいていく歌。

I'm looking out at all the scene
from up here where the bridges lean
But nothing opens up the way
I'd hoped it would, so I don't stay

A cold and bitter wind blows by
and ripples where the waters lie
I have no answer in me now
so I just hurry past somehow

The sky will clear, the clouds will come
and roll back in, and I go numb
I come and go and never see
how all of it keeps changing me

I'm searching all around the stream
to find the castle's shadow gleam
and cursing at the glaring light
that blocks away and clouds my sight

Right away my eyes go searching for you, and somewhere down beneath
A caked-on residue of mind lies deep and stuck inside of me
And it's always pulling me on ahead before I ever come to know
And it beats my time to nothing as I go

I'm lying back across the grass
and looking up to watch it pass
but still the awkward way I lie
is all I feel, I don't know why

I pick a pebble up to throw
and wind back, ready, here we go
but I can never leave the ground
I stay, to earth and body bound

What am I chasing after, tell me why I'm always in a rush?
The meaning of the question, that is something I can well allow
But even so I'm lost adrift and wavering, still even now
And I just keep on blaming all of time for everything somehow

But even as the autumn slips away and winter passes too
The ones who dress themselves in colors, they're the beautiful and true
And the castle's figure trembling on the surface of the flowing stream
Depends on you who watch it gleam
