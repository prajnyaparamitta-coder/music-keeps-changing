Pale Mermaid

Listen on Suno

波間を淡く漂う美が、静けさへとゆるやかに溶けていく一瞬を切り取った歌。

Lowered eyes, a tilted face
lashes lift with quiet grace
just a hint of playful pride
hiding where the smiles reside

Strands of hair across your lips
draw me in with subtle tricks
every breath so sharp and clear
pulls the moment closer here

At my fingertips a sugar sigh
soft and warm go drifting by
dancing light begins to play
glimmering and slips away

As if testing how far it goes
you lay your body slow and close
like the reach of endless sea
stretching out to cover me

Smoothly sliding, droplets fall
every one seems breathing all
as though life was hiding there
in the shimmer of the air

Breathing still, a certain form
guided by the gentle swarm
of rolling waves that pull apart
then conceal your fading heart

Soon we’re caught in one slow swell
drawn inside where new things dwell
everywhere new pulses start
blooming soft and coming to rest, finally at rest
