A Girl Goes to Town Alone

Listen on Suno

どんなに不安でも、どんな理不尽が待ち受けていようと、運命を引き受けようと覚悟を決めた少女の歌。

On a winter’s day
When the light’s astray
When the smell of snow is thick and drifting through the air

With a scarf too long
That does not belong
Hanging loose and bare for all the watching eyes to see her there

Looking all around
Like she’s lost and found
Glancing side to side, her hair in twin tails, through the town

“This is just my duty now”
So she takes a vow
Heading toward the streets alone

Hemmed in all around by a crowd of towering giants
Pressed until she can hardly draw a single breath at all
And without a thought at all she lifts her timid eyes
And timidly she throws a glance right back

Before she knows
the way the sunlight goes
When the smell of night soaks deep and spreads through everything

Losing sight of where
losing sight of there
Hurrying her steps along the way through all the doubt and daze she goes

Even though she tried
thought she’d slipped aside
Unseen weapons paying no mind to her will and her heart

Dressed as a moment
of pure coincidence
it comes swinging down on her
